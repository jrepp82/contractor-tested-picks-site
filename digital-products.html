How monthly delivery works

After a successful first membership payment, Prestige matches your tier to the verified onboarding toolkit and current monthly member ZIP and sends both to the checkout email. On a successfully paid renewal, only the new monthly member drop is delivered. The automated fulfillment watcher checks subscription and month before sending so a renewal is not mistaken for the original purchase.

Billing: memberships renew monthly until canceled. For billing changes or cancellation support, email Jason@prestigeremodelingwi.com. Previously delivered files remain yours under the membership license; cancellation stops future drops after paid access ends.
