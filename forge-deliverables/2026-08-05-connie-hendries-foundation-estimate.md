## Technical conclusion

A factory-finished foundation panel can supply the insulation and visible concrete/stucco-style appearance without a separate field-applied skim coat. This is the recommended approach because it reduces weather-dependent curing, produces a more predictable finish, and avoids a second coating operation.

The field-finished alternative uses new rigid foundation insulation plus a compatible reinforced protective coating. The repair/re-skim alternative is conditional and should only be selected after field verification confirms the existing insulation remains firmly bonded and structurally serviceable.

## Pricing basis checked
