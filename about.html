Built behind the work

Prestige Select turns hands-on experience into better buying and business decisions.

The brand connects contractor-focused gear, welding and garage markets, collectibles supplies, Prestige DigiTools, monthly contractor resources, resale inventory and Prestige Remodeling into one commerce system.

The operator behind it

Built from real work, not a studio.

Jason Reppen is a Manitowoc-area contractor with decades of remodeling, construction, welding, fabrication and inspection experience, including Certified Welding Inspector work.

That background is used to organize products around real use cases instead of random trending items. Categories marked as experience-backed reflect areas of firsthand trade or hobby experience; that does not claim firsthand use of every individual marketplace listing.

The standard

Useful, transparent and connected.

Prestige Select is designed around practical usefulness, clear affiliate disclosure and direct paths into the rest of the Prestige network. A shopper researching tools can move into live tracked market searches. A contractor can move into digital operating systems or recurring resources. A local homeowner can move directly into Prestige Remodeling.
