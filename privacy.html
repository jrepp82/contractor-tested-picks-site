Privacy Notice

This notice explains how information is handled across the Prestige website and connected services used for Prestige Select shopping, inquiries, email subscriptions, checkout and digital-product delivery.

Information you choose to provide

When you submit a Prestige contact form, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, project location and project or product details. Prestige may use that information to respond, prepare an estimate, schedule work, provide customer support or answer a product question.

Email subscriptions

Prestige uses Klaviyo for optional marketing-email subscriptions tied to the free contractor resources and commerce funnel. If you subscribe, Klaviyo may receive the email address and optional first name you provide, along with basic signup-source information. Marketing emails include an unsubscribe option.

Digital-product checkout and fulfillment

Digital-product payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe processes checkout and payment information under its own privacy and security practices. Prestige receives transaction information needed to identify and fulfill the order, such as purchaser email, product purchased, payment status and Stripe transaction identifiers.

For products delivered by email, the fulfillment system checks for qualifying completed paid orders and sends the corresponding private product file to the purchaser email. Google Drive may be used for private product-file storage and Gmail for delivery.

Affiliate and external links

Prestige Select contains links to third-party websites, including eBay. Those sites may collect information under their own privacy policies. Prestige does not control their cookies, tracking or data practices.

Affiliate tracking

Some outbound eBay links contain eBay Partner Network campaign and custom tracking parameters so eligible purchases can be attributed to Prestige Select. These parameters do not increase the price paid by the customer.

Analytics and conversion measurement

The website contains code for measuring page views and actions such as affiliate clicks, digital-product checkout clicks, email signups and lead submissions when an analytics service is configured and active. The site may also use local browser storage for limited on-site diagnostic or click-tracking functions.

Third-party services

Services used by the current Prestige workflow may include Vercel for hosting, GitHub for source management, FormSubmit for contact-form delivery, Klaviyo for email subscriptions, Stripe for checkout and payments, Google Drive for private product-file storage, Gmail for fulfillment email, Supabase for protected delivery functions and eBay for marketplace and affiliate destinations.

Retention and access

Business inquiries, order records and fulfillment messages may be retained as reasonably needed for customer service, order verification, accounting, fraud prevention and business records.
