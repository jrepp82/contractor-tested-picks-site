Privacy Notice

This notice explains how information is handled on the Prestige / Contractor Tested Picks website and through the services used for inquiries, email subscriptions, checkout and digital-product delivery.

Information you choose to provide

When you submit the website contact form, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, project location and project or product details. The form is submitted through a third-party form-delivery service so the inquiry can be delivered to Prestige. Prestige may use that information to respond, prepare an estimate, schedule work, provide customer support or answer a product question.

Email subscriptions

Contractor Tested Picks uses Klaviyo for optional marketing-email subscriptions. If you subscribe, Klaviyo may receive the email address and optional first name you provide, along with basic signup-source information such as the page where the form was submitted. The current list uses a confirmation step before marketing emails are sent. Marketing emails include an unsubscribe option.

Digital-product checkout and fulfillment

Digital-product payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects and processes the checkout and payment information needed to complete the transaction under its own privacy and security practices. Prestige receives transaction information needed to identify and fulfill the order, such as the purchaser email, product purchased, payment status and Stripe order/session identifiers.

For products delivered by email, the fulfillment system checks for completed paid Stripe orders and sends the corresponding private product file to the purchaser email. Google Drive is used for private storage of certain product files and Gmail is used for email delivery. Prestige Essentials may instead redirect a successful Stripe checkout to a private product-download page.

Affiliate and external links

This website contains links to third-party websites, including eBay. Those sites may collect information under their own privacy policies. Prestige does not control their cookies, tracking or data practices.

Affiliate tracking

Some outbound eBay links contain eBay Partner Network campaign and custom tracking parameters so eligible purchases can be attributed to Contractor Tested Picks. These parameters do not increase the price paid by the customer.

Analytics and conversion measurement

The website contains code for measuring page views and actions such as affiliate clicks, digital-product checkout clicks, email signups and lead submissions when an analytics service is configured and active. Analytics providers may process device, browser, page, referral and interaction information according to their own policies. The site may also use local browser storage for limited on-site diagnostic or click-tracking functions.

Third-party services

Services used by the current website and sales workflow may include Vercel for website hosting, GitHub for website source management, FormSubmit for contact-form delivery, Klaviyo for email subscriptions, Stripe for checkout and payments, Google Drive for private product-file storage, Gmail for fulfillment email, Supabase for protected delivery functions, and eBay for marketplace and affiliate destinations. Each provider may process information according to its own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and access

Business inquiries, order records and fulfillment messages may be retained as reasonably needed for customer service, order verification, accounting, fraud prevention and business records. If you want to ask what information Prestige has about you, request a correction, or ask for deletion where applicable, contact Prestige using the information below.
